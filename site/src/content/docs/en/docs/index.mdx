Welcome to the Baram documentation. Baram is a lightweight, beautiful WYSIWYG markdown
editor with AI integration and bidirectional links — the pages below cover everything from
your first document to publishing a plugin of your own.

## Start here

New to Baram? These steps take you from an empty window to a linked set of notes.

<Steps>

1. **[Install Baram](/baram/en/docs/getting-started/#installation)** — download the build for
   your platform, or build it from source.

2. **[Open a folder](/baram/en/docs/getting-started/#first-launch)** — point Baram at a folder
   of markdown files, or start a fresh document. The file tree appears on the left; the right
   sidebar starts closed, so most of the window is writing space.

3. **[Write your first document](/baram/en/docs/editing/files-and-tabs/)** — files, tabs, and
   auto-save a moment after you stop typing, with a dot on the tab until it lands.

4. **[Format without leaving the keyboard](/baram/en/docs/editing/slash-commands-and-toolbars/)** —
   slash commands insert any block; the markdown syntax fades as you leave it.

5. **[Link your notes together](/baram/en/docs/linking/wikilinks-and-tags/)** — wikilinks, tags,
   and backlinks are what turn a folder of files into something you can navigate.

6. **[Make the folder a vault](/baram/en/docs/workspace/vaults-and-approval/)** — a vault adds
   vault-level settings, its own journal directory, and links across vaults.

7. **[Turn on AI](/baram/en/docs/ai/setup-inline-and-ghost-text/)** *(optional)* — bring your own
   provider and key, or keep everything local with Ollama.

</Steps>
